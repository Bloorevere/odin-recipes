What are we doing?

For this recipe you are going to be making quick, easy, and flavorful Pasta. This is a recipe that can be made quick, easy, and scaled to the level that you would like very easily. As a mattor of fact, I personally make this recipe for my large family of eight people on a regular basis!

Ingredients

  • 1 Lb of ground shrimp
  • head of lettuce
  • 1 pasta seasoning packet

Steps

  1. Brown Shrimp in pan, and cook pasta
  2. Add pasta seasoning
  3. enjoy!
